Nobody noticed I’d stopped

I was training in three places with three apps: calisthenics at Leamington on one, yoga on another and the gym on a third, with a WhatsApp group on top. Each had its own login and its own way of booking, so I put off opening any of them.

The sets my coach gave me for the days in between ended up in WhatsApp, my notes or a screenshot, and that’s where they stayed, so the work between classes never happened. I also kept paying for a membership I wasn’t using, and the money went out whether I went or not.

My last yoga class was in January 2024, and nobody from the studio got in touch. I’d gone quiet, and from the studio’s side a member who’s gone quiet looks just like one who’s busy.

Kinmova is the app I wanted then: one week with every class and every set in it, wherever they come from, and a studio that can see someone drifting while there’s still time to send a nudge.

Luke, founder of Kinmova

Questions

Is Kinmova ready to use?

Not yet. We’re building it now and opening it to founding studios first. We’ll tell you when your studio’s place is ready, and your three free months start on the day you go live, not the day you sign up.

What happens after the three months?

It’s £15 a month, plus 2% + 20p on each card payment your members make through Kinmova. You decide whether to stay, and if you don’t want to carry on there’s nothing to cancel.

Do my members pay anything?

No. Members use the app for free, at your studio and at any other studio on Kinmova.

Can you move my members over from my current system?

Yes. Send us an export from the system you use now and we’ll set up your members, memberships and class packs for you.

Do you take direct debit as well as cards?

Yes, both, and the money your members pay goes to your bank account.

Does it work if I’m the only coach?

Yes. It’s built for studios and clubs with roughly 30 to 150 members, and it works just as well if you run every class yourself.

What do you need from a founding studio?

Half an hour a month to tell us what’s working and what isn’t, and a bit of patience while we fix what you find.
