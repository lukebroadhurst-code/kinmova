Questions

Is Kinmova ready to use?

Not yet. We’re building it now and opening it to founding studios first. We’ll tell you when your studio’s place is ready, and your three free months start on the day you go live, not the day you sign up.

What happens after the three months?

You decide whether to stay. We’ll tell you the monthly price well before your free months end, and if you don’t want to carry on there’s nothing to cancel.

Do my members pay anything?

No. Members use the app for free, at your studio and at any other studio on Kinmova.

Can you move my members over from my current system?

Yes. Send us an export from the system you use now and we’ll set up your members, memberships and class packs for you.

Do you take direct debit as well as cards?

Yes, both, and the money your members pay goes to your bank account.

Does it work if I’m the only coach?

Yes. It’s built for studios and clubs with roughly 30 to 150 members, and it works just as well if you run every class yourself.

What do you need from a founding studio?

Half an hour a month to tell us what’s working and what isn’t, and a bit of patience while we fix what you find.
