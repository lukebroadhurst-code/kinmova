The Kinmova brand

Everything here comes from one idea. A member’s week is seven days: two in the studio with their class, and five in between. Kinmova is for those five, so the brand is built from a week of dots, black for class days and magenta for the days someone trained on their own.

The logo

The name is always lowercase in the logo, followed or topped by the week. In a sentence it’s written Kinmova, with a capital K.

Side by side, for headers, letters and slides

On black, class days turn sand

Stacked, where there’s more height than width

The week on its own, only where the name is already close by

Keep clear space the width of one dot all the way round. Don’t go smaller than 120px wide for the side-by-side logo; below that, use the icon. Never reorder or recolour the dots, set the name in another typeface, stretch it, outline it, or put it on a photograph without a solid panel behind.

How Kinmova sounds

Like the one who notices: the regular at your club who knows everyone’s name, clocks who’s missing, says well done without a fuss, and isn’t above a dry remark.

Noticing is what Kinmova does for a living, since it shows owners who’s kept it up and who’s slipping, so the voice notices too. Write in full sentences that run on naturally, joined with “so”, “and” and “but”, rather than a string of short fragments. Talk to owners as “you” and about their people as members.

It notices the small stuff

Name the day, the set and the number.

“Sam held the dead hang for 45 seconds on Wednesday.”

It’s dry, never hyped

Understatement does the cheering. No exclamation marks, no fire emojis.

“Not bad for a wet Wednesday.”

It says the awkward thing kindly

Say who’s slipping out loud, without guilt-tripping anyone.

“Tom’s gone quiet. It’s nine days since his last class.”

It thinks in weeks

This week, the days in between, by Thursday. Never goals, journeys or transformations.

“Two things to do before Thursday’s Pull.”

Our own words

  • “gone quiet”, for a member drifting off
  • “kept it up”, for training between classes
  • the days in between
  • a nudge
  • the regulars
  • members, never users, clients or customers

Words we never use

  • journey, transform, transformation
  • crush it, smash it, beast mode
  • unlock, empower, elevate, game-changer
  • seamless, all-in-one
  • “community” as a buzzword
  • “not just X, it’s Y”
  • Note to an owner

    Three of your Pull class have gone quiet this week. Tom’s been away longest, at nine days. Want to send him a nudge?

  • Note to a member

    Dani’s set you two things for today, and one of them is the dead hangs you’ve been putting off.

  • A member’s personal best

    45 seconds on the dead hang, which is more than double what you managed in September.

  • An empty week

    Nothing booked yet this week. Thursday’s Pull has three places left.

  • A post for owners

    Genuine question for studio owners: how do you spot a member who’s gone quiet before they cancel?
