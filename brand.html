The Kinmova brand

Everything here comes from one idea. A member’s week is seven days: two in the studio with their class, and five in between. Kinmova is for those five, so the brand is built from a week of dots, black for class days and magenta for the days someone trained on their own.

The logo

The name is always lowercase in the logo, followed or topped by the week. In a sentence it’s written Kinmova, with a capital K.

Side by side, for headers, letters and slides

On black, class days turn sand

Stacked, where there’s more height than width

The week on its own, only where the name is already close by

Keep clear space the width of one dot all the way round. Don’t go smaller than 120px wide for the side-by-side logo; below that, use the icon. Never reorder or recolour the dots, set the name in another typeface, stretch it, outline it, or put it on a photograph without a solid panel behind.

How Kinmova sounds

Like a good coach talking to a studio owner after the last class of the night: warm, plain and specific, with a bit of humour when it fits.

Write in full sentences that run on naturally, joined with “so”, “and” and “but”, rather than a string of short fragments. Name real days, times and numbers, so it’s “Wednesday” and “23 of 32” rather than “regularly” and “most”. Talk to the owner as “you” and about their people as members. One joke at most, and never at a member’s expense.

Words we use

  • members
  • class, book, cancel
  • between classes
  • trained, sets, a personal best
  • your studio, your club
  • well done, a nudge
  • founding studios

Words we avoid

  • users, clients or customers, for members
  • unlock, empower, elevate, supercharge
  • seamless, all-in-one, game-changer
  • fitness journey, crush your goals
  • revolution, disrupt
  • “not just X, it’s Y”
